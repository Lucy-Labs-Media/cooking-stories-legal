Delete My Data

Word Search: Cooking Stories, by Lucy Labs Media. Last updated November 25, 2026.

Most of your data is stored only on your device, so removing the game removes it. Some information is held by Google and our service providers, and we can help with that too.

1. Delete data on your device

Your puzzle progress, unlocked levels, recipe collection, settings, and purchase status are saved on your device.

  1. Close the game.
  2. To keep the game but erase its data, open Settings → Apps → Word Search: Cooking Stories → Storage → Clear storage.
  3. To remove everything, uninstall the game.

Menu names can differ between phones. If you can't find a step, search your phone's settings for "Apps" and the game's name.

2. Purchases

The Baker's Permanent Pass is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google keeps the record of your purchase, so uninstalling the game doesn't cancel or refund it.

To request a refund, use Google Play's refund process at support.google.com/googleplay. To restore a purchase on a new device, sign in to the same Google account and tap Restore purchases in the game.

3. Analytics data

The game uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to understand how it's played. We don't link it to your name or email address. If you want us to delete analytics information connected to your device, email us with your phone model and the approximate date you played. We'll do our best to find and delete it.

4. Ask us

For anything not covered here, or to ask us to delete information we hold about you, email:

lucylabsmedia@gmail.com

Put "Delete my data" in the subject line. We aim to reply within 30 days.
