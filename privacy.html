Privacy Policy

Word Search: Cooking Stories, by Lucy Labs Media. Last updated November 25, 2026.

Lucy Labs Media ("we," "us") makes Word Search: Cooking Stories ("the game"). This policy explains what the game handles and why. We have tried to write it in plain language.

1. Summary

  • The game has no ads, and it contains no advertising software.
  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game uses Firebase Analytics to understand how it's played.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. RevenueCat checks whether your purchase is active.

2. Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, unlocked levels, recipe collection, settings, and purchase status are saved on your device. We don't send this information to our own servers. Uninstalling the game removes it. See Delete My Data for steps.

3. Purchases

The Baker's Permanent Pass is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google keeps its own record of the purchase, and its privacy policy applies to that information. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether the pass is active on your device. RevenueCat receives an identifier generated by the game and purchase details needed to confirm the pass. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Analytics

The game uses Firebase Analytics, a service from Google, to record how the game is used. This can include events such as levels started and completed, use of the first-play guide, and purchase screens. It can also include technical details about your device and app version. We don't use this information to show ads. We don't record names, email addresses, purchase tokens, or the text you type. See Google's Firebase privacy information.

5. Feedback

If you use the feedback link in the game, you're sent to a Google Form. Anything you type there is sent to us, and we use it only to improve the game. Don't include information you don't want us to keep.

6. Service providers

We use Google (Google Play, Firebase), RevenueCat, and GitHub Pages, which hosts this site. Each handles information under its own privacy policy. We don't sell your information.

7. Advertising

The game shows no advertisements and doesn't include an advertising SDK. The game doesn't use the advertising ID.

8. Children

The game isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Clear the game's storage or uninstall it to delete the data on your device.
  • Ask for a refund of a purchase through Google Play's refund process.
  • Ask us to delete analytics information connected to your device. See Delete My Data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
